About Me!

I’m Quincy Crittendon, a quarterback from Decatur, Alabama, and a former Samford University quarterback. During my career, I was honored to be recognized with distinctions such as the Walter Payton Award watch list and Preseason Second Team All-SoCon.

I also had the opportunity to contribute to one of the most successful stretches in Samford football history, including the program’s first outright Southern Conference championship and an FCS Playoff run. In 2024, I led the Southern Conference in passing yards with 2,689, ranked second in completion percentage at 70.3%, and recorded four 300-yard passing games.

Along the way, I threw for a career-high 456 yards against Western Carolina and 378 yards with four touchdowns against nationally ranked Mercer. Those accomplishments mean a lot to me, but even more important is what they taught me about preparation, leadership, consistency, and performing in key moments.

That is the perspective I now bring into quarterback training as I help athletes sharpen their skills, grow in confidence, and prepare for the next level.
